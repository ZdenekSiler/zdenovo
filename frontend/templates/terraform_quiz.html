Core Features

Objective-by-Objective Practice

A sidebar of all eight exam objectives, each with a live score and a progress bar. Work one domain at a time instead of shuffling everything together, and finish each objective with a summary of exactly what you missed.

Explanations on Every Answer

Answer and the correct option lights up immediately, with a written explanation of why it’s right and where the distractors go wrong. 205 questions, 205 explanations — the point is to learn the material, not to farm a score.

Timed Mock Exam

57 questions drawn proportionally from every objective, a 60-minute countdown, and no feedback until you finish. Pass mark is 70%, matching the real exam. The clock turns red under five minutes and auto-submits at zero.

Per-Objective Breakdown

The mock exam scores each objective separately, so you can see which domain sank you rather than just a single percentage. Below the breakdown, every missed question is replayed with your answer, the right one, and the explanation.

Anti-Memorisation Shuffling

Toggles to shuffle answer order and question order, on by default for answers. Run an objective twice and the options move, so you end up learning the material instead of the position of option C.

Nothing Leaves the Browser

No accounts, no backend, no analytics, no cookies. Every question and every answer lives in one static HTML file; all state is in-memory JavaScript. Close the tab and it’s gone — nothing is recorded anywhere.

Exam Objectives

The question bank follows HashiCorp’s eight published objectives for the 004 blueprint. Each is drillable on its own, and the mock exam samples from all of them in proportion to their size.
